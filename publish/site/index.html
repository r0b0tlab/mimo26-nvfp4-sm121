Quantization

Weights converted from the vendor MXFP4 checkpoint with NVIDIA ModelOpt public APIs. No third-party quantization code or configs.

What was quantized

  • BaseXiaomiMiMo/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL @ 5711b268, MIT. 90 files, 177.8 GB.
  • ToolModelOpt main @ 7159c01d. Full SHA 7159c01d9d909ca2431db6332363f07b2137ac09.
  • ExpertsRouted MoE experts converted MXFP4 to NVFP4, E2M1, block size 16, FP8 block scales.
  • LinearsAttention and dense linears NVFP4 where a per-tensor FP8 scale helped, BF16 otherwise.
  • ActivationsPer-tensor scales. Policy peer_headroom, chosen by runtime-style QDQ scoring (nvfp4_act_relmse).
  • KVFP8 e4m3 with calibrated per-layer scales. Both TP ranks carry the same scale vector.
  • Left BF16555 auxiliary tensors: audio tokenizer, vision tower, nextn. Draft DFlash weights unchanged.

How the scales were chosen

  • Calibration512 by 512 rows from an internal corpus. Post-calibration NLL 1.5317.
  • GateEach layer’s chosen scale must be at most 1.10× the best candidate on that layer. Worst observed ratio 1.056.
  • ExportMXFP4 routed experts rewritten to NVFP4. 0 non-exact blocks among 9.46 billion compared. Input scales live in their own shard so the activation policy can be rewritten without touching the 175 GB weight tree.
  • Integrity24 shards, 36,096 quantized linears, 0 mismatches. Fabric mirror of 66 files, hashes matched.
  • Publishedr0b0tlab/MiMo-V2.6-Flash-RL-NVFP4. Checkpoint, hf_quant_config.json, kv_scales.json, model card.

Method. QDQ scoring compares candidate activation scales on the runtime quantize-dequantize path, not on a proxy MSE of the weights alone. The conversion check is a block-level exactness count against the expected NVFP4 packing, then a separate shard walk that hashes every quantized linear.

SM121 patch

What it changes. On the NVFP4 MoE path, SGLang allocates w13_blockscale_swizzled and w2_blockscale_swizzled even when the runner is Marlin. Marlin never reads them. It repacks w13_weight_scale and w2_weight_scale in prepare_moe_nvfp4_layer_for_marlin. The patch adds is_marlin() to the same skip already used for TRT-LLM and MegaMoE, and sets those two tensors to null.

Why it is the SM121 path. Packed MiMo experts are served with --moe-runner-backend marlin. Skipping the unused swizzle frees about 9 GiB per rank (1/16 byte per expert weight element at EP=2 on this 309B-A15B MoE). That memory is what the KV pool uses. DFlash still loads through the stock stacked q/k/v map. No second loader image.
